Record once. Fix it only if you want to.

Record

Open a normal website tab, press Start recording, and work as usual. Tab audio is captured with the video. You can close the popup and the recording keeps going.

The zoom triggers on clicks, double clicks, focusing a field, and typing. Scrolling zooms back out.

Adjust

Every zoom is saved as a block on a timeline in the editing studio. Drag one, change its level, point it somewhere else, delete it, or add your own at the playhead. Pick a background, trim the ends, and export.

If the first pass is already right, skip the studio.

Deliver

Download the video at 720p, 1080p or 4K. Or press Stop and share to upload a 720p copy and get a link you can paste into a ticket, a pull request or a chat.

Questions.

Does my recording get uploaded?

No. The video is stored on your computer. The only upload happens when you press Stop and share or Create share link, and then only that 720p copy goes to Cloudinary.

Why does exporting take as long as the video?

The studio renders your edit by playing the video through in real time, so a two minute recording takes about two minutes to export. Keep the editor tab open until it finishes. Trimming works the same way and saves a new copy.

Can I stop it zooming on some things?

Yes. Turn off zoom on click or zoom while typing and regenerate, or delete individual blocks from the timeline. You can also add zooms by hand wherever you want one.

Can I record a Chrome settings page or a new tab?

No. Chrome does not allow extensions to capture its own pages. Open a normal website tab and start from there.

What exactly does it record?

The tab you start on, plus that tab's audio. Your other tabs, other windows and the rest of your screen are never captured.

Do I have to edit the video?

No. The zooms are placed for you when you press stop, so you can download it as it is. The editor is there if you want to move a zoom, change the background or trim.

Can I change how the video looks?

Yes. Choose from six backgrounds (midnight, sunset, ocean, forest, graphite and snow), then adjust the padding, corner radius and shadow. You can also click the preview to aim a zoom exactly where you want it.

What quality and format do I get?

Downloads come as 720p, 1080p or 4K WebM files. Higher settings use a higher bitrate (2.5, 5 and 12 Mbit/s), so the files are bigger. WebM opens in Chrome and most editors and players. If you need MP4, run it through any video converter.

Does trimming damage my original?

No. Trimming saves a new copy and keeps the original, so you can always go back.

Do screenshots cost anything?

No. PNG screenshots are free and unlimited, including a zoomed one that centres on your last click or the field you were typing in.

What if a share link fails to upload?

It doesn't cost you anything. A link only counts against your allowance after the upload succeeds, so you can simply try again.
